# LocalLens – Privacy Policy

**Version:** Pilot v1.0  
**Effective date:** To be confirmed before public launch  
**Contact:** locallens.org@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

LocalLens is a travel planning application that helps users organize trips, discover destinations, and share travel experiences.

This Privacy Policy describes the personal information that may be processed when users access LocalLens, including its Community Pilot features.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, LocalLens may process:

- **Account information:** Account identifiers, email addresses, and authentication information.
- **Profile information:** Display names, profile preferences, and information voluntarily provided by users.
- **Travel planning information:** Saved places, itineraries, trip details, and group collaboration data.
- **Community content:** Reviews, ratings, reports, and related moderation records.
- **Media:** Images voluntarily uploaded when supported Community features are enabled.
- **Location information:** Location information provided or permitted by users when location-based features are available.
- **Technical information:** Information necessary for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

Not all categories are collected from every user. Some Community features may remain disabled during the Pilot.

## 3. How Information Is Used

LocalLens may process information to:

- Authenticate users and maintain accounts.
- Provide travel planning and collaboration features.
- Display user-submitted Community content when publication is enabled.
- Review reports, moderate content, and prevent misuse.
- Maintain application security and investigate technical issues.
- Respond to support and privacy requests.

## 4. Community Visibility

Content submitted for public Community publication may become visible to other users after applicable checks and moderation.

Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in public reviews or uploaded media.

Private account information and private trip information are not intended to become public merely because Community features are enabled.

## 5. Data Storage and Service Providers

LocalLens uses technical service providers, including Supabase, for application infrastructure such as authentication, databases, and storage.

Information may be processed on infrastructure operated by these providers, subject to their applicable security and contractual arrangements.

## 6. Data Retention

LocalLens intends to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to operational requirements and applicable law.

Specific retention periods for Community content, uploaded media, and verification information must be established and confirmed before the relevant features are activated.

## 7. User Choices and Requests

Users may contact LocalLens to request information about their personal data, seek correction or deletion where applicable, or raise privacy concerns.

Requests can be sent to:

**locallens.org@gmail.com**

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable legal requirements and the technical capabilities of the service.

## 8. Security

LocalLens uses access controls and other technical measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

LocalLens may update this Privacy Policy when features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Material changes will be communicated where required.
