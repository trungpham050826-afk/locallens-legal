## 1. Introduction

LocalLens is a travel planning application that helps users organize trips, discover destinations, and share travel experiences.

LocalLens is developed and operated by an individual as a personal project, without a separate legal entity. References to "LocalLens" in this Policy mean the project and its individual operator. The individual operator handles privacy inquiries and data requests through [locallens.org@gmail.com](mailto:locallens.org@gmail.com).

This Privacy Policy describes information processed when using LocalLens and its available Community features. This revision applies from the effective time above; publication does not itself activate features or retention operations.

Community may include Places, Reviews and ratings, explicitly selected photo/video attachments, Reports, Blocks, Moderation and a feed of approved content. Availability depends on release checks and account eligibility. Private memories and review attachments are separate; their existence does not permit automatic Community uploads. GPS/partner verification and Smart Discover are not represented as active by this notice.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, LocalLens may process:

- **Account and profile information:** Account identifiers, email addresses, authentication-related records, display names, and preferences you provide. Authentication accounts and public Community profiles are distinct records.
- **Travel information:** Saved places, itineraries, trip details, and shared Group Trip content when those features are used.
- **Private records:** Personal reviews, journals, and photo/video references or files used by private memory features. A device-local record is not automatically a cloud-synchronized record.
- **Community records:** Review text, ratings, place references, selected review details, public profile identifiers, publication consent and its policy version, review versions, and submission status.
- **Selected Community media:** Only the files/captions you explicitly choose, their restricted staging copies, processed derivatives/thumbnails, identifiers and processing/moderation status. Files can include metadata before processing; visible or audible personal details may remain in derivatives.
- **Self-attested visits, where enabled:** Your selected place and supplied date or trip link. These are user statements, not independent proof. GPS/partner evidence is not collected merely by making a self-attested statement.
- **Safety records:** Report reasons and explanations, references to reported content, block relationships, moderation decisions, and relevant audit or anti-abuse records.
- **Technical and support information:** Operational metadata needed for authentication, security, troubleshooting, and handling requests, including information you send by email.

Not every category applies to every user. Disabled features do not begin collecting evidence just because they are described here. Self-attestation is not consent to GPS tracking. Other location-based app features may process information you supply or permit under their separate controls.

## 3. How Information Is Used

LocalLens may process information to:

- Authenticate users and maintain accounts.
- Provide travel planning and collaboration features.
- Process an explicit Community submission and display approved public content when publication is enabled for the relevant users.
- Review reports, moderate content, and prevent misuse.
- Maintain application security and investigate technical issues.
- Handle blocks, retain relevant reported content, and maintain moderation records.
- Respond to support and privacy requests, including checking the scope and ownership of requested data.

## 4. Community Visibility

Approved Community reviews may show your public display name, review text, rating, place, selected details and approved media, and publication-related dates to all eligible authenticated users with access to Community. Do not assume approved content is visible only to the moderator or to your Group Trip members. A previously approved version may remain visible while an edit is under review.

Opening Community, completing a trip, or keeping a private review does not itself publish private content. Publication requires a separate submission. Account email addresses, private journals, and private trip records are not part of the intended public review projection; details you put in your review text can still become public.

Reports and block records are not public Community content. Authorized staff and infrastructure processes may access relevant records to handle moderation, security, and privacy requests. Blocking changes visibility within supported Community screens; it does not erase either user's data or prevent other people from seeing published content.

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information or another person's information without permission. Others may copy content they can see; withdrawing a review cannot guarantee deletion of screenshots or copies outside LocalLens's control.

## 5. Data Storage and Service Providers

LocalLens uses Supabase for authentication, databases and supported Storage features. Selected Community media goes to restricted Supabase staging; the LocalLens worker on Railway validates it and uses FFmpeg to create derivatives/thumbnails. Processing infrastructure and authorized moderators can access selected submitted content as required; processing does not imply publication approval. The author cannot approve their own review. Private local files are not automatically Community uploads. The public policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages; visiting it involves request processing by that hosting provider, separate from app Community records.

Information processed by service providers is subject to their infrastructure and applicable arrangements. Hosting locations, any cross-border processing and provider backup retention have not been verified in this notice. No particular data-residency or backup-erasure commitment is made here.

Some Trips and Saved Places use device-shared storage and may remain visible to another person using the same app installation. Private reviews and their media use account-scoped local storage, which separates their in-app account views but is not a guarantee against someone with device-level access. Signing out does not necessarily erase stored files. Shared Group Trip data is available according to that feature's existing membership and permissions, not Community publication rules.

## 6. Data Retention

The proposed setting is **180 days for eligible stored Community review content after the review is marked deleted**, subject to approval and an approved cleanup process. This is not an automatic expiry date for an active review, account, trip, or every category of personal information. Unpublishing a review is not necessarily the same as marking it deleted.

The Community retention and account-erasure workflow is **not fully automated**. The proposed period does not itself trigger permanent erasure, and eligible records may remain beyond 180 days pending processing. Removing content from public display and physically deleting stored content are separate steps.

Unresolved reports can require preservation of the particular reported content. Review records, moderation history, reports, block records, anti-abuse records, and request receipts do not necessarily follow the same deletion schedule. Some records may remain to investigate disputes, maintain security, preserve necessary record relationships, or meet applicable legal requirements. Retention for each category and the handling of these exceptions must be confirmed; this draft does not assign them a universal 180-day limit.

Deleting an authentication account does not by itself establish that related Community records, shared trip data, or device-local files have been erased. Provider backups and copies outside LocalLens's control are separate from deletion in the active application database; their erasure timing is not verified here.

Community Storage cleanup is manual and eligibility-based, with at least the current 24-hour technical grace, checks for references, unresolved reports and holds, and verification of object absence before recording completion. This is not a promise to erase every file within 24 hours. Failed jobs remain pending for inspection and approved retry. The permanent Data Operations Owner supervises retention, deletion requests, Storage cleanup and minimal completion evidence; no cleanup scheduler or fully automated account erasure is represented as deployed. Read the [Community Media Notice](community-media.html).

## 7. User Choices and Requests

Use available review controls to edit, withdraw, or delete your content where supported. You may also request information, correction, content deletion, or account deletion by emailing [locallens.org@gmail.com](mailto:locallens.org@gmail.com). Rights available to you depend on applicable law.

Specify whether the request concerns a review, your Community profile, your authentication account, device-local records, or another category. Send the request from your account email where possible and provide only the references needed to identify the records. Do not send passwords, login codes, or unnecessary identity documents.

LocalLens may need to verify ownership and clarify the request before taking action. Requests currently require review and may involve manual steps; this draft does not promise an immediate or fully automated account-erasure process. Any response obligations depend on applicable law rather than an invented processing deadline.

The response should explain the scope processed, any retained records and reasons, and any remaining device-side steps. An email request cannot directly erase files on your device or another person's copies. Signing out or uninstalling does not delete the server-side account. Do not assume that clearing one category removes all account or Group Trip data; requests involving shared records need separate review.

## 8. Security

LocalLens uses access controls and other technical measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, including separate controls for public projections and private records. These measures do not make private content public merely because it is stored alongside Community operations.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

LocalLens may update this Privacy Policy when features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Approved revisions should identify their version and effective date. Material changes will be communicated where required. This revision applies prospectively from the effective time above, following publication.
