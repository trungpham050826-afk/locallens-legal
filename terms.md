## 3. User Responsibilities

Keep your account credentials secure. You are responsible for the accuracy of the content you submit and for having the rights and permissions needed to share it. Reviews should describe your own experience; do not present an unverified visit as independently confirmed.

Users must not publish:

- False or misleading reviews.
- Harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory content.
- Spam, unauthorized advertising, or fraudulent content.
- Personal information belonging to others without permission.
- Content that infringes intellectual property rights.
- Impersonation, manipulated ratings, or coordinated abuse of reviews or reports.

Do not include passwords, identity documents, precise private whereabouts, or other sensitive information in public reviews. Do not use reports to harass other users or submit fabricated allegations.
